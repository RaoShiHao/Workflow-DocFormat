# Formatting Target notes

The following supplements `template.docx` and can help you interpret the formatting targets in the template.

## Section

- **Cover section**: Defines the portrait cover page for the report title, logo, year, and report facts.
- **Contents section**: Defines pages containing the table of contents and report navigation entries.
- **Opening message section**: Defines the chairman and CEO letter section before the report chapters.
- **Highlights section**: Defines the front-matter annual highlights and indicator summary section.
- **Group overview section**: Defines the chapter section covering group profile, strategy, footprint, and operating model.
- **Segment review section**: Defines operating segment review chapters with comparable narrative, tables, charts, and images.
- **Governance section**: Defines governance, board, committee, leadership, ethics, and remuneration content.
- **Risk and sustainability section**: Defines risk management, sustainability, environmental, people, safety, and impact chapters.
- **Financial statements section**: Defines the consolidated primary financial statements and related financial review content.
- **Financial notes section**: Defines detailed accounting notes, disclosure schedules, judgments, and supporting explanations.
- **Annex section**: Defines the supplementary schedules and appendix figures section, which hosts tables or figures.
- **Glossary and contact section**: Defines glossary, registered-details, website, and investor contact pages.
- **Legal notice section**: Defines forward-looking statements, basis notes, and legal disclaimer pages.

## Heading paragraph

- **Cover title**: Used for the principal Rivermark Holdings report title on the cover page.
- **Heading 1**: Used for every body level-one chapter heading, including Group Overview, financial notes, and back annex chapters where applicable.
- **Heading 2**: Used for every body level-two section heading beneath a major chapter.
- **Heading 3**: Used for every body level-three subsection heading across operating, governance, sustainability, and note content.
- **Heading 4**: Used for every body level-four topic heading, including the many repeated disclosure and performance topics.
- **Heading 5**: Used for every body level-five disclosure heading in detailed financial notes and other deep structures.
- **Heading 6**: Used for every body level-six definition, interpretation, schedule, and judgment heading.

## Body paragraph

- **Contents title**: Used for the visible title of the contents page.
- **Contents entry level 1**: Used for top-level report chapter entries in the contents navigation.
- **Contents entry level 2**: Used for indented subchapter and note-group entries in the contents navigation.
- **Letter salutation**: Used for the opening salutation in the chairman and CEO letter.
- **Letter body**: Used for narrative paragraphs in the chairman and CEO letter.
- **Letter signature**: Used for signer names, titles, and dates closing the leadership letter.
- **Highlight lead**: Used for the short lead statement introducing annual achievements and key indicators.
- **Executive summary**: Used for high-level summary prose opening overview, segment, financial, governance, risk, or sustainability discussions.
- **Segment narrative**: Used for operating segment performance, market, driver, capacity, and outlook narratives.
- **Body text**: Used for standard business, governance, sustainability, financial, and annex explanatory prose.
- **Bulleted list**: Used for unordered initiatives, risks, actions, controls, and operating priorities.
- **Numbered list**: Used for ordered procedures, policy sequences, and structured note explanations.
- **Figure caption**: Used beneath charts, diagrams, maps, and operating or leadership photographs.
- **Table caption**: Used above or below performance, statement, disclosure, roster, and annex tables.
- **Financial statement lead**: Used to introduce a primary financial statement or a major financial note block.
- **Disclosure note**: Used for dense accounting, assurance, legal, and financial-note disclosure paragraphs.
- **Source note**: Used for source, basis, scope, and methodology notes beneath tables or figures.
- **Annex heading**: Used for visible titles of supplementary schedules and appendix figure groups in the back matter.
- **Glossary entry**: Used for term-and-definition entries in the glossary and abbreviation pages.
- **Contact information**: Used for registered details, addresses, websites, and investor-relations contact paragraphs.
- **Legal notice**: Used for forward-looking statements, legal disclaimers, reporting basis notes, and authorization language.
- **Cover subtitle**: Year, tagline, or report subtitle on the cover.

## Table-cell paragraph

- **Header cell**: Used for column and row header text in performance, statement, disclosure, roster, and annex tables.
- **Data cell**: Used for ordinary values and label-value content in table cells, including merged cover labels.
- **Stub cell**: Used for row-name cells containing financial line items, segment names, or schedule labels.
- **Note cell**: Used for explanatory note rows beneath performance, statement, and disclosure tables.

## Run

- **Lead bold**: Used to emphasize label-before-colon leads inside summary, narrative, disclosure, and glossary paragraphs.
- **Superscript note mark**: Used for footnote and cross-reference marks attached to report prose, source notes, and statement leads.
- **Italic term**: Used for defined metrics, report terms, and publication titles within narrative and disclosure text.
- **Underline blank**: Used for signature blanks or fill-in lines appearing in signatures, contacts, or legal forms.

## Table

- **Cover information table**: Used on the cover for report year, reporting period, and other label-value facts.
- **Performance data table**: Used for group, segment, operating, sustainability, shareholder, and KPI performance comparisons.
- **Financial statement table**: Used for consolidated statements of financial position, profit or loss, cash flows, and changes in equity.
- **Disclosure schedule table**: Used for detailed accounting schedules, reconciliations, ownership, tax, risk, and note disclosures.
- **Personnel roster table**: Used for board and management roster information including names, roles, and organization columns.

## Image

- **Cover logo**: Used for the Rivermark Holdings corporate logo or report emblem on the cover.
- **Performance chart**: Used for bar, line, or pie charts showing annual, segment, financial, sustainability, or shareholder metrics.
- **Structure diagram**: Used for group structure, ownership, organization, and governance diagrams.
- **Process diagram**: Used for business model, value creation, value chain, and reporting process diagrams.
- **Operations photo**: Used for facilities, projects, operating teams, and field activity photographs in segment chapters.
- **Portrait photo**: Used for board members, executives, or featured leadership portraits.
- **Footprint map**: Used for maps showing operating regions, sites, markets, or segment footprint.
